What Christine Offers

Services

With a specialty in Myofascial Therapy and Swedish massage, every session is tailored to your body's needs on that day.

  • Christine's Specialty

    Myofascial Therapy

    Gentle, sustained pressure into the connective tissue (fascia) to release deep restrictions, ease chronic pain, and restore freedom of movement.

  • Christine's Specialty

    Swedish Integration

    A flowing, full-body massage using gentle to medium pressure. Perfect for stress relief, improved circulation, and deep relaxation.

  • Deep Tissue

    Targeted work on deeper muscle layers to release chronic tension, address problem areas, and restore ease of movement.

  • Hot Stone Therapy

    Smooth, warmed stones melt away tension and coax tight muscles into release — deeply soothing, grounding, and restorative.

  • Energy Work

    Subtle, intuitive work to balance and restore the body's energy. Can be woven into any treatment or experienced as a session all its own.
